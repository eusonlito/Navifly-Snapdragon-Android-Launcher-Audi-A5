THEME 1 — "Horizonte" (map-first, glass, minimal, linear instead of circular).
The map fills the entire screen edge to edge, as the background. All data floats over it in frosted dark-glass rounded panels with soft shadows. No round dials at all: speed is a huge digital number in a left glass card with a thin horizontal progress bar under it; RPM is a horizontal segmented bar with the red zone at the end and the gear shown as a big letter chip beside it. Trip values are a single slim glass strip along the bottom with small uppercase labels. Fuel is a thin vertical bar. Commands are a floating pill-shaped dock at the top centre. Palette: graphite glass, white text, single cyan accent (#22D3EE), red only for redline. Calm, premium, lots of breathing room.
