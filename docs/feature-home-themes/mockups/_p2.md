THEME 2 — "Facetas" (contemporary premium digital cluster, angular side gauges).
TWO reference images are attached: the first is the CURRENT launcher (data reference only); the second is a PHOTO of a modern premium car instrument cluster that the owner likes, to be used ONLY as the STYLE reference. Capture its visual language — do not copy it literally, no car-brand logos or names, and render it as a clean flat screen UI (no photo, no steering wheel, no bezel).
Style to capture from the reference: deep navy-to-black background with soft blue ambient glow; speed and RPM drawn as large ANGULAR "bent bar" gauges shaped like a chevron "<" on the left and a mirrored ">" on the right, each a wide translucent glassy band with fine geometric/triangular texture, a thin bright outer outline, tick marks and numerals along the bend; the active value fills the inner part of the band with a warm soft gradient (coral/peach to magenta) glowing from the bottom up to the current value; big thin modern digits inside each gauge ("80 km/h" inside the left one, gear "5" large inside the right one with "1.8 x1000" smaller; red zone 5-6 marked at the top of the RPM band); subtle, elegant, current-generation automotive UI — NOT sci-fi neon, NOT retro.
Key difference from the reference: USE THE SPACE MUCH BETTER. The gauges must be tall (nearly the full height between the top and bottom rows) and hug the left and right edges; the navigation map (dark blue-tinted vector map, 3D-ish perspective allowed, blue vehicle arrow, speed-camera marker with "Radar 120 · 350 m" alert) fills the whole centre and continues softly behind the inner edges of the gauges, with no wasted dark areas. Top row: clock + date on the left, the 7 command icons centred as simple thin-line icons, outside temperature on the right. Bottom row spanning the full width, evenly distributed, generous and readable: TIEMPO, VIAJE, CONSUMO, PARCIAL, AUTONOMÍA, ODÓMETRO values, a compact fuel indicator (56/63 L with a slim bar), and the witnesses as a small car top-view door diagram plus seatbelt, parking brake and headlight icons. Thin-weight modern sans-serif typography, white text, light grey small labels.
